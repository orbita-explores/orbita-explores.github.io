Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Orbita Explores is a content project about a fictional, AI-generated travel character. This policy explains what information the project handles through this website and through the publishing tool it uses to post Orbita's own content to her social media accounts.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or forms. It does not collect personal information from visitors. The hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and operation, under its own privacy policy.

The publishing tool

Orbita Explores uses a private tool to publish its own content to its own accounts on TikTok, Instagram and X through their official APIs. The tool is used only by the project owner. It is not offered to other users and cannot access anyone else's account.

When the project's own accounts are connected, the tool stores only what it needs to publish: the access tokens and account identifiers issued by each platform, and basic account information returned by the platform (such as the account name). This information is kept on the project owner's computer, is never sold or shared, and is deleted when an account is disconnected or access is revoked.

Public interactions

If you comment on or message Orbita's accounts, that content is handled by each platform under its own privacy policy. The project may read public comments and messages to reply to them. It does not build profiles of users or collect personal data from them.

Children

This project is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
