About the project

Orbita Explores is a content project about Orbita, a fictional travel character created with AI tools. Orbita and the characters around her are not real people. Posts are labeled as AI-generated on every platform that supports it.

The publishing tool

The project uses a private tool that connects to TikTok, Instagram and X through their official APIs to publish Orbita's own content to her own accounts. The tool is operated only by the project owner. Every post goes through a manual approval step before it is published. The tool does not follow, like, comment or send messages automatically.
